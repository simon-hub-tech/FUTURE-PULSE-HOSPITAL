General Medicine

Our general medicine service provides routine medical consultations, diagnosis, and treatment for common illnesses. We also support preventive care to help patients maintain good health.

  • Medical consultations and diagnosis
  • Routine health check-ups
  • Management of common illnesses
images/service-2.jpg

Maternity and Child Care

We support mothers and children through every stage of care, from pregnancy to early childhood. Our team focuses on the health, comfort, and wellbeing of both mothers and their babies.

  • Antenatal and postnatal care
  • Maternal health consultations
  • Child wellness checks and immunisation
images/service-3.jpg

Emergency Care

Our emergency care service provides prompt assessment and treatment for urgent medical conditions. We aim to stabilise patients quickly and provide appropriate care when every moment matters.

  • Emergency medical assessment
  • First aid and stabilisation
  • Urgent treatment and referrals
